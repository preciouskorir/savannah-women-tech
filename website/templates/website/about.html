About Savannah Women Tech

Empowering women and young people across Africa through technology, education, mentorship, and innovation.

Our Mission

To empower women and young people across Africa and the diaspora with digital skills, leadership training, mentorship, and entrepreneurship opportunities that enable them to thrive in an increasingly digital and AI-driven world.

Our Vision

About the Founder

Sheila Korir

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

"Talent is everywhere. Opportunity should be too."

I believe that every woman, every young person, and every aspiring entrepreneur deserves the opportunity to learn, grow, lead, and succeed, regardless of where they begin.

My journey from Kenya to the United Kingdom has given me a unique perspective on both the opportunities and challenges that shape people's lives. Throughout my career in higher education, employability, apprenticeship programmes, stakeholder engagement, and community development, I have worked closely with learners, graduates, and professionals, helping them navigate their personal and professional growth. Along the way, I witnessed a common reality: countless talented individuals were held back not by a lack of ability, but by limited access to opportunities, mentorship, networks, and confidence.

That realisation inspired me to establish Savannah Women Tech.

Savannah Women Tech was founded with a vision to become more than a training organisation. It is a movement dedicated to empowering women and young people with the knowledge, skills, confidence, and opportunities they need to thrive in an increasingly digital and AI-driven world.

Our work focuses on digital skills development, leadership, entrepreneurship, mentorship, innovation, and emerging technologies. We are creating an inclusive ecosystem where individuals can unlock their potential, build meaningful connections, and access opportunities that transform their lives and communities.

As Founder and Chief Executive Officer, I am committed to bridging the gap between talent and opportunity across Africa and the diaspora. Through collaboration, innovation, and purpose-driven leadership, my goal is to build one of Africa's leading women-focused technology and empowerment organisations: one that equips future leaders, supports entrepreneurs, and creates lasting social and economic impact.

I envision a future where women are not simply participants in technology and innovation but leaders shaping its future. A future where young people have the confidence, skills, and support to pursue ambitious goals, build successful careers and businesses, and become catalysts for positive change within their communities.

This is more than an organisation.
It is a mission to unlock potential, inspire leadership, and create opportunities that empower individuals to build brighter futures for themselves and generations to come.

My Purpose

To empower women and young people to become confident leaders, innovators, entrepreneurs, and changemakers who transform their communities and shape the future.

My Vision

To build one of Africa's most impactful women-focused technology and empowerment ecosystems, reaching thousands of lives through education, innovation, mentorship, entrepreneurship, and opportunity.

"Success is not only measured by what we achieve ourselves, but by the opportunities we create for others to thrive." Sheila Korir
