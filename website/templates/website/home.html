Our Mission: Technology as a Path to Empowerment

Savannah Women Tech bridges the digital gender gap across Africa by providing world-class technology education, mentorship, and entrepreneurship opportunities that transform communities and build sustainable futures.

We believe every woman and girl deserves access to the tools, knowledge, and networks that drive the digital economy.
